Case study 01 — building operational infrastructure

Standing up a multi-service delivery platform before launch

DropX · Multi-service delivery · Lagos · Operations Manager · 2026 · Remote

The challenge

DropX was preparing its Lagos delivery operation and needed its processes, customer, vendor and rider workflows, support structures, financial controls and risk procedures organised — and proven — before going live.

My role

I worked across Operations, Customer Support, Finance, Rider & Vendor operations, Compliance and Product — documenting workflows, field-testing the delivery experience, and coordinating the departmental reviews that got the operation launch-ready.

What I built across the operation

Nine workstreams, from front-line delivery to financial and legal governance.

01 · Process

Operations manual & SOPs

Core processes and workflows turned into practical, department-level SOPs.

02 · Support

Customer support ops

Support processes, FAQs, escalation structures, ticket handling and service standards.

03 · Finance

Finance & accounting ops

Refunds, reconciliation, wallet management, deductions and financial controls.

04 · Riders

Rider operations

Onboarding, documentation, safety expectations, delivery procedures and controls.

05 · Vendors

Vendor operations

Onboarding, fulfilment, settlements, refunds and prohibited-item requirements.

06 · Customers

Customer operations

Orders, cancellations, refunds, disputes, delivery issues and wallet credits.

07 · Compliance

Regulatory research

Lagos courier licensing and LASAA permit rules for riders, branding and boxes.

08 · Governance

Legal & policy stack

Customer, vendor, rider, refund, data-protection, breach and cookie documents.

09 · Risk

Operational risk review

Liability, refund exposure, fraud, insurance and data-breach exposure.

Governance

Coordinating cross-functional review.

Documentation moved through department gates, then overlapping policies were consolidated — not duplicated.

Gate 01

Finance & accounts

Flagged the need to define numerical approval and reconciliation thresholds before sign-off.

Gate 02

IT & data protection

Reviewed data access, OTP/PIN, GPS, retention, deletion, breach response and third parties.

Gate 03

Management

Prepared the review pack and flagged items needing management and legal confirmation.

Then

Consolidation

Compared new policies against existing docs and merged overlaps into a single source.

The review surfaced exposure around refund shortfalls, rider recovery, vendor deductions, goods-in-transit insurance, payment disputes and reconciliation controls — each tied to a clear owner before launch.
