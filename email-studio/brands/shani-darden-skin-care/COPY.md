## Diagnosis

Segment: a footer subscriber who wants retinol results and has been burned or scared off by irritation. Awareness stage 2: knows Shani by reputation (Hollywood esthetician), not convinced her retinol is gentle enough for them. Task: a limitation removed (irritation). Sophistication 3 (labelled assumption: every brand sells a retinol): the mechanism is named, encapsulated 1% retinol, and the new thing is Shani's slow start. One performance: start retinol without the irritation. Belief built on: Shani knows skin. Belief routed around: retinol has to hurt to work. Belief left alone: any medical comparison to prescriptions beyond reviewers' words. Route: central (\$75). Defensible claim: "this upgraded formula delivers 1% retinol more effectively, with less irritation", the 2-week study on 39 subjects, 4.8 from 118 reviews. Structure: a how-to welcome (the plan is the persuasion). Levers, three: authority (Shani), a step-by-step plan (commitment in small nights), social proof (sensitive-skin reviewers, not perfect: Libby K.'s 4 stars is shown). Left out: Cherry and Afterpay finance lines, devices, the quiz.
