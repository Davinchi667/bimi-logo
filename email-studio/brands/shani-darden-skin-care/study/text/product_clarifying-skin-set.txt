WHO IS SHANI DARDEN

Considered the secret behind Hollywood’s most flawless faces, expert esthetician Shani Darden sets herself apart with her results-oriented approach to skin care.

What it is

A complete daily routine for blemish-prone skin that helps visibly reduce breakouts while supporting a healthy skin barrier. Featuring Shani's Cleansing Serum, Rescue Serum, Weightless Oil-Free Moisturizer, and Control Corrective Oil-Free SPF, this four-step routine gently cleanses, treats, hydrates, and protects for skin that looks clearer, calmer, and more balanced.

 

What's Included
Cleansing Serum 120ML A gentle cleanser that removes dirt and makeup without stripping skin of natural oils.
Rescue Serum 30ML Powered by 10% Azelaic Acid and soothing botanicals to visibly reduce blemishes, calm redness, minimize the look of pores, and improve overall skin clarity.
Weightless Oil-Free Moisturizer 75ML Lightweight, oil-free hydration that helps balance moisture levels while supporting a healthy skin barrier.
Control Corrective Oil-Free SPF 74ML A broad-spectrum SPF 30 moisturizer that protects against UV damage while delivering shine-free hydration ideal for oily and blemish-prone skin.

How to use
Your Daily Routine for Clear, Balanced Skin

Morning and evening, begin by cleansing with Cleansing Serum. Apply 1–2 pumps of Rescue Serum to clean, dry skin to help visibly reduce blemishes and calm redness. Follow with Weightless Oil-Free Moisturizer to replenish hydration without clogging pores. In the morning, finish with Control Corrective Oil-Free SPF as the final step to protect skin from daily UV exposure.
