WHO IS SHANI DARDEN

Considered the secret behind Hollywood’s most flawless faces, expert esthetician Shani Darden sets herself apart with her results-oriented approach to skin care.

What it is

A targeted treatment duo pairing two of skincare’s most powerful ingredients: azelaic acid and retinol. Rescue Serum with 10% Azelaic Complex visibly improves blemishes, redness, dark spots, and post-blemish marks, while Retinol Reform® with 1% Encapsulated Retinol improves the look of fine lines and wrinkles, visibly refines texture, and supports healthy collagen for firmer-looking skin.

 

What's Included
Rescue Serum with 10% Azelaic Complex 30ML Powered by a 10% Azelaic Complex with soothing oat, aloe, and licorice extracts to visibly improve blemishes, redness, dark spots, post-blemish marks, and uneven tone for clearer, more even-looking skin.
Retinol Reform® with 1% Encapsulated Retinol 30ML A high-strength retinol treatment featuring advanced slow-release technology to visibly refine texture, improve the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles, and support firmer, smoother, more radiant-looking skin.

How to use
Shani’s Go-To Routine

AM + PM: Apply Rescue Serum in the morning after cleansing and follow with moisturizer and SPF. In the evening, apply Retinol Reform to clean, dry skin and follow with moisturizer. Alternate Evenings: Apply Rescue Serum one evening and Retinol Reform the next, alternating based on your skin’s tolerance. If you’re new to retinol, start slowly and increase frequency as your skin acclimates. Always wear SPF during the day.
