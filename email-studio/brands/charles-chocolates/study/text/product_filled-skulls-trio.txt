Sample each of our limited edition filled skulls: Dark chocolate with spiced chocolate caramel, milk chocolate with s'mores, and Ruby (the 4th type of chocolate) with candied apple caramel

Dark chocolate: 65% bittersweet shell, spiced chocolate caramel made with coconut cream, chili and cinnamon
Milk chocolate: graham cracker ganache and vanilla marshmallow
Garnet: milk and white chocolate shell, candied apple caramel made with real apple puree

The set comes tied with our black signature ribbon. A wicked gift for yourself or a fun gift for a loved one.

3 boxes of 5 skulls, 3 oz (85g) each. Best enjoyed within three months.

Allergens: Garnet contains milk. Milk chocolate contains milk, wheat and eggs. Please see each box's label.

Manufactured in a facility that also processes products containing tree nuts, peanuts, milk, eggs, wheat and soy.
