## Diagnosis

Segment: a new subscriber who took the skin quiz popup and got SEEKER15, has not ordered. Awareness stage 2: drawn by the brand's plant story, unsure how to use the products (one reviewer: "I didn't know I had to mix it with hydrosol"). Task: a new way to use it (the oil & water press), a limitation removed (how-to). Sophistication 3 (labelled assumption: "clean" and "organic" skin care is crowded), so the method is the mechanism. One performance: oil and water, pressed together. Belief built on: plants, gently. Belief routed around: face oils feel greasy. Left alone: any treatment claim. Route: central (\$80.98 for the two-bottle start). Defensible claim: "Our remarkable HydroSouls pressed together with oil serums, moisturize, hydrate, and nurture all skin without stripping the skin's protective barrier." 15% off the first purchase with SEEKER15; Harvest Ritual \$135 vs \$187.46 of full sizes. Structure: how-to welcome with an offer. Levers, three: the code (reason: the quiz they took), the method (named mechanism), value (Harvest Ritual's own compare-at). Left out: SMS, consultations, refills, the gift-with-purchase tiers beyond one footnote line.
