Oscar Henao - Red Honey 

After eight years away in the textile industry, he came back to the farm during the pandemic and started learning what it could do. This coffee goes through a long process. Oscar lets the whole cherry ferment for five full days before depulping it and carefully drying it. All that time pays off in the cup. It's clean and fruity, with a little chocolate at the end.
