## Diagnosis

Segment: a visitor who read "Sign up for 15% off" in the footer (and could not use the form) or who signs up once it works. Awareness stage 2: they know single-serve coffee and the brand; doubt is "is a pour-over bag fussy, and is it better than a pod". Stage 2 task: a limitation removed (time and fuss: 90 seconds, a mug and hot water). Sophistication 3 (labelled assumption: pods and instant own "quick"), so the mechanism is named: a pre-filled filter that hooks over the mug, with Vietnamese robusta. One performance: a real pour-over in 90 seconds. Belief built on: pour-over tastes better than pods. Belief routed around: pour-over takes time and kit. Belief left alone: caffeine/acidity as benefits (one site line, bracket kept). Defensible claim: Classic Black, \$16.00 for 8, Rated 5.0 out of 5 stars from 29 reviews, 100% would recommend, ready in 90 seconds, 15% off as the footer words it. Structure: offer ad with a demonstration (the brew clock) and a proof stack. Levers, three: the discount (reason: signing up), social proof (5.0 / 29 / 100% and four reviews), liking through the founder (Debbie and the 2X farmer pay). Left out: Subscribe and Save 15% (a second discount), rewards tiers, Shark Tank line (inside an unattributed press quote).
