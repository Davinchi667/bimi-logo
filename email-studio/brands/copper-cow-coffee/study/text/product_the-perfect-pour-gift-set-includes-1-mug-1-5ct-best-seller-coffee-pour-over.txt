We're excited to announce The Perfect Pour Gift Set! Whether you’re gifting a coffee lover or treating yourself, this set makes it easy to try the best of Copper Cow Coffee. Enjoy our best selling flavors, from our Classic Black to our flavored options made with real herbs and spices! 

What’s Included

1 Coffeeist Mug – a sturdy, minimalist ceramic mug designed for the perfect pour

1 5ct Best Brews – includes our fan-favorites (2 Classic, 1 Churro, 1 Vanilla, 1 Salted Caramel)

Real Flavor, Really:
Each blend starts with premium Vietnamese robusta coffee, dark-roasted for its naturally rich, chocolatey flavor. We grind real ingredients — like whole cinnamon sticks, vanilla beans, and salt — directly into the coffee so they infuse as you brew. Real flavor, really.

How to Brew 

Hook filter over your mug.

Pour 1 oz hot water, wait 30 sec.

Add 3–12 oz more hot water depending on desired strength (3 oz for Vietnamese style, up to 12 oz for an Americano) pro tip, we made a mug just for this!

Enjoy black or with creamer.
⏱️ Ready in 90 seconds!
