For: Claude (claude.ai) building from COPY.md, and Design Studio for the three GIFs. 600 px wide, 7 sections, 2,560 px tall, 3 motion moments.

## Signature concept: the ear map

Broken English sells earrings "Sold as a single", has a collection called The Ear Edit, and its Trouver page says singles are "ideal for building the perfect ear stack". S3 draws one ear in a fine jeweller's line and pins five real singles to it (lobe, second lobe, upper lobe, helix, upper helix), each on a leader line to its cut-out, designer, name and price. Beside the ear, a receipt-style running total adds each piece as it lands and ticks the \$250 line with the first one. The offer condition stops being small print and becomes part of the drawing. Proof inside the concept: five live prices from the .js (\$590.00, \$550.00, \$400.00, \$430.00, \$570.00), "Sold as a single", the metals from each listing, the site's own ear-stack sentence, and the offer's "\$250 or more" condition with its exclusions line. There are no customer reviews on the site; the trust slot is Laura's quotes (S4) and a Client Care chat module (S5).

## What NOT to do

- No reviews of any kind, no stars, no "loved by" lines: the site has none.

- No three white cards; no colour-swap template; no blush-pink jewelry cliché or marble backgrounds.

- No best-seller claim, no incense or claw clips in the offer area.

- Never show the 10% without "\$250 or more" beside it; never imply every piece qualifies (exclusions apply).

- No code lettered; no code in subject or preview; no "Up to 60% Off" (hidden banner).

- No on-model ear photography invented or generated; the ear is a line drawing.

- No em dash in any layer. No emoji.
